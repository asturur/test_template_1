# Step 5G — resident stores across a cache-line boundary

Read `AGENTS.md`, `docs/status.md`, `docs/cpu-integrated-5f.md` and `docs/cpu-store-postindex.md`. Use the existing master checkout. The fixed 5F build uses the same five numeric multiplier encodings with their widths made explicit in commit `8e70b1e`. Freeze that commit as the control. The accepted 5F performance comparison and stock donor measurements are already recorded.

1. Trace both original tail fills across all scored windows. Locate each line transition by physical tag, word and accepted transaction. Count the clocks from actual operand dequeue to probe availability, accepted request, physical write and retirement. Identify who owns the last request of the old line and the first of the next. Reconcile the 63/64 crossings and the 250 newly exposed empty clocks in fill8 against wall time. Determine which part of the estimated 126/128 clocks could move useful work earlier. Stop here if it cannot plausibly recover at least 54 clocks in the halfword tail and 66 clocks in the byte tail.
2. If the traces support it, try one registered change to prepare or retain the next resident line's probe result. Revalidate permission, tag and ownership before each actual acceptance. Keep the serial fallback whenever evidence is unavailable or stale. Preserve same-slot and same-line hazards, pending-store ordering, late data, aborts, IRQ boundaries and MMIO isolation. Keep the existing cache shape and physical PLL. Price any additional read ports, state or tag comparisons against the matched 5F fit. Avoid a new unregistered tag-to-issue path.
3. Compare the two original tails to 1,332 and 1,647 clocks at both memory profiles. Retain only if their medians reach at most 1,278 and 1,581 respectively, both guard regions and every checked byte match, the warmed 64-store stream still completes in 71 clocks or better, and no other established kernel or JPEG regresses by more than 0.5%. Check crossing, alias/dirty, probe collision, rejected permissions and address/base preservation at a fault; exercise interrupts while stores are pending. Use existing suites and rerun any affected originals with their normal repeats and negative controls. Keep simulations serial and remove only your own compiler outputs after preserving receipts.
4. Write the decision, cycle schedules, resource estimate, safety evidence and remaining bottleneck under docs. If qualified and retained, reconcile just the candidate, tests and upstream patches into master. Otherwise leave the qualified 5F pipeline in production. One experiment ends this step. The donor, physical phone and CI deployment are outside this experiment.
